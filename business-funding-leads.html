Why under-640 credit is the feature, not the flaw

Your funders price deposits and receivables, not FICO. A bank already told these owners no. An SBA broker can’t help them. A funding shop can. So when your closer calls, he isn’t competing with a bank, and he isn’t competing with a term-loan broker either.

Merchants who take an advance tend to take more than one. Industry figures put it at about two and a half advances a year, with roughly six in ten looking for more capital within six months. One funded lead is a renewal, not a single commission.

The other half, so nobody feels lied to

First deals on this paper run smaller. Funders size a sub-prime first advance at about 70 to 80 percent of one month’s deposits, so a $25,000-a-month merchant is roughly an $18,000 to $20,000 deal, not $50,000. Clawback risk runs higher. Commission often sits at the low end of the 5 to 15 percent range. If you only fund 700-plus credit, we are not your vendor, and we’d rather say so here than after you’ve paid.

Questions funding shops ask before they test

“Exclusive is never really exclusive.”

Agreed. That’s why we don’t ask you to trust the word. Each lead has a hash tied to your buyer ID and a consent record with our page and the time. If a merchant we sent reaches you from another vendor within 30 days, five leads on us.

“Sub-580 credit won’t fund.”

Your funders price deposits, not FICO. A merchant under 580 with $20,000 a month in real deposits can’t get a bank loan, so you’re not competing with one. Published fund rates on fresh leads run around five percent in real cases and higher by vendor claim. The test lets you measure yours. We won’t quote a number we can’t show.

“I get ‘exclusive’ leads for $40 and transfers for $35.”

Then buy those and track cost per funded deal for 30 days. If a $40 lead goes to five shops, your contact rate is the problem, not the price. Ours cost more because the owner proved the number, the deposits clear $20,000 a month, and the lead went to you alone. We’ll talk price on the call, with your numbers in front of us.

“You’re new. You’ll be gone in three months.”

Fair. That’s why it’s 25 leads, prepaid, no contract, and you can stop any day. We generate business-funding leads from our own ads and deliver them to funders through our buyer portal. You’ll see the portal before you pay.

“Are these declined files from another funder?”

No. Every lead is a first-party submission from our own ads and our own form. The TrustedForm certificate shows the page it came from and when. We buy no data and take no declined files.

“Your volume won’t feed my floor.”

Right now you’d get a capped daily allotment and you’d be one of a handful of buyers. Volume moves with our ad spend, and early buyers keep priority as it grows. If you need fifty a day today, we’re not your vendor yet.

“What happens when a lead is bad?”

Wrong or dead number, bouncing email, duplicate, under the $20,000 bar, or the owner says they never filled out a form: flag it within 48 hours with your call log and it’s replaced, up to 15 percent of the order. Didn’t answer or didn’t close isn’t a return.

“Am I safe calling these?”

Every owner confirmed the number by text code, the email was checked as deliverable, and the owner agreed to consent language on the form, which TrustedForm records. They also get a text and an email with your company name before you call, so the call is expected. You’re still responsible for your own dialer, DNC scrub and state rules, and we say that in writing.
